# Lumenfold Candle Co. — support FAQ

Lumenfold Candle Co. is a 3-person studio making hand-poured soy candles, wax melts and reed diffusers. Webshop: https://lumenfold.example. Support email: support@lumenfold.example. We answer within 1 working day (Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00 CET).

## Orders and shipping
- Orders placed before 13:00 CET on a working day ship the same day; later orders ship the next working day.
- Domestic shipping: 2–4 working days, free over €45, otherwise €4.90.
- EU shipping: 4–8 working days, €9.90, free over €80. We don't ship outside the EU yet.
- Every order gets a tracking link by email when it ships. Order status page: https://lumenfold.example/orders
- Candles are made in small batches; a sold-out scent is usually back within 2–3 weeks. Restock alerts: https://lumenfold.example/restock

## Damaged, wrong or missing items
- Send a photo of the damaged item and the box within 14 days of delivery. We send a free replacement or a full refund, your choice. No need to return broken glass.
- Wrong item: we send the right one and a prepaid return label.
- Missing parcel: if tracking shows delivered but nothing arrived, we open a carrier claim and resend after 3 working days.

## Returns and refunds
- Unused, unlit items can be returned within 30 days. Return form: https://lumenfold.example/returns
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days after the return arrives.
- Lit candles can't be returned unless faulty (tunnelling in the first 2 burns, cracked glass, wick problems).

## Candle care
- First burn: let the wax melt to the edge (about 2–3 hours) to avoid tunnelling.
- Trim the wick to 5 mm before each burn. Burn max 4 hours at a time.
- Burn time: 180 g candle about 40 hours, 300 g candle about 65 hours.
- All candles are vegan soy wax with cotton wicks; fragrance oils are phthalate-free. Ingredient sheets: https://lumenfold.example/ingredients

- Corporate gifts and custom labels from 25 units; lead time 3–4 weeks. A team member sends a quote within 2 working days.
- Wedding favours (mini candles) from 50 units, custom label included.
